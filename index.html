What MemoryCare does

Small helps that make a big difference

Each feature is designed to be simple, calm and easy to understand, with no clutter and no confusing menus.

  • Familiar faces

    Photos of loved ones with their name and relationship: “This is Sarah, your daughter.” One tap to call them.

  • Gentle reminders

    Friendly spoken and on-screen prompts for medication, meals, appointments and the day’s plans.

  • Today at a glance

    A clear, always-on view of the day, date, time and what’s happening next, to help ease disorientation.

  • Memory book

    A shared album of photos, voice notes and stories that family can add to, for reminiscing together.

  • Safe at home & out

    Optional location sharing and a large “Help me get home” button that guides the way and alerts family.

  • Calming moments

    Favorite music, soothing sounds and simple breathing guides for moments of anxiety or restlessness.

Designed for the person

Simple enough to use on a hard day

We designed MemoryCare around how people with dementia actually experience technology. Every screen has one clear purpose, big buttons and plain words.

  • Extra-large text and high-contrast colors
  • Read-aloud for every screen
  • No ads, pop-ups or surprise updates to the layout
  • Works with tablets, phones and smart displays

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is MemoryCare for?

MemoryCare is for people living with early- to mid-stage dementia or memory loss, and the family members and caregivers who support them.

Does the person with dementia need to set anything up?

No. A family member or caregiver handles setup from their own phone. The person using MemoryCare simply sees a calm, ready-to-use home screen.

What devices does it work on?

MemoryCare works on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. A large tablet on a stand works especially well at home.
