Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is MemoryCare for?

MemoryCare is for people living with dementia or memory loss, and for the family members and caregivers who support them.

Does the person with dementia need to set anything up?

No. A family member or caregiver adds medications, schedules and contacts from their own phone. The person using MemoryCare simply sees a calm, ready-to-use home screen.

What happens when the SOS button is pressed?

Their caregivers are notified straight away with their location. The screen also offers a one-tap button to call emergency services, and an “I am OK, cancel” button if it was pressed by mistake.

How do fall alerts work?

If a possible fall is detected, MemoryCare asks “Are you OK?” with two large buttons: “I’m OK” and “I need help”. If there’s no reply before the countdown ends, caregivers are alerted.

Who can see their information?

Check-ins, tasks, game scores and location are shared with the caregivers in their care circle. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.

What devices does it work on?

MemoryCare is available for iPhone and Android phones.

Is MemoryCare a medical device?

No. MemoryCare is a daily-living support tool and does not diagnose or treat any condition. It doesn’t replace emergency services. Always follow the advice of your health professionals.
