What MemoryCare won’t do

  • It isn’t a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything, and the games aren’t a test.
  • It doesn’t replace 000. SOS alerts family. In an emergency, call 000.
  • It needs power and signal. Alerts only go out if the phone is on, charged and connected.
  • It won’t catch every fall. Fall detection can miss a fall, or raise an alarm when nothing happened.

Frequently asked questions

Who is it for?

People living with dementia or memory loss, and the family members and carers who support them.

Does the person with dementia have to set anything up?

No. A family member or carer adds the medicines, tasks and contacts from their own phone.

Who gets told when SOS is pressed?

The caregivers in their care circle, with their location. The same screen has a button to call emergency services, and “I am OK, cancel” for accidental presses.

Who can see their information?

The caregivers you add. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and who else handles it.

What phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android.
