The short version

  • MemoryCare handles health information, such as medications, mood check-ins, falls and brain game scores. We treat it as sensitive and only collect it with consent.
  • Information is shared with the caregivers in the care circle and the service providers who help us run the app. We never sell personal information.
  • You can see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
  • If something goes wrong, you can complain to us and then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

The rest of this policy explains these points in full.

1. About this policy

MemoryCare is operated by [Legal entity name] (ABN [ABN]) (“MemoryCare”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information when you use the MemoryCare mobile app, our website and any related services (together, the “Service”).

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Where they apply, we also comply with state and territory health records laws, such as the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW).

In this policy:

  • the person receiving care means the person living with dementia or memory loss who uses the MemoryCare home screen; and
  • a caregiver means a family member, friend or care worker who uses MemoryCare to support them, and the care circle is the group of caregivers linked to that person.

2. Using MemoryCare for someone else

Dementia can affect a person’s ability to make decisions about their personal information. MemoryCare is usually set up by a caregiver, who enters information about the person receiving care.

If you set up or manage MemoryCare for another person, you confirm that:

  • the person has agreed to you doing so; or, if they can’t give that consent, you are legally authorised to consent for them (for example, as their enduring guardian, attorney under a power of attorney, or “person responsible” under the law of your state or territory);
  • you will act in their best interests, and involve them in decisions about MemoryCare as much as they are able to be involved; and
  • you have told any other people whose details you add (such as other caregivers and emergency contacts) that you are sharing their details with us.

We may ask you to confirm your authority. Where it’s practical, we design MemoryCare so the person receiving care can see what is being shared about them. For example, the check-in screen says it is “Shared with your caregivers”.

3. What we collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide the Service.

About the person receiving care

  • Profile details: name, preferred name, photo (if added) and time zone.
  • Health information: medications, doses and schedules, whether reminders are marked as taken or done, daily mood check-ins and any notes, SOS requests, fall alerts and responses, and brain game results and trends.
  • Tasks and routines: daily tasks such as appointments, walks or calls.
  • Location: the device’s location, including live location while the location feature is turned on, and location sent with an SOS or fall alert.
  • Motion data: readings from the device’s motion sensors used to detect a possible fall.

About caregivers and contacts

  • Name, email address, phone number, relationship to the person receiving care, and account login details.
  • Settings and actions you take in the app, such as adding a medication or viewing location.

Technical information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, notification tokens, crash reports and diagnostic logs.

Health information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We only collect it with consent (given by the person or by someone authorised to consent for them, see section 2), or where the law otherwise allows.

You don’t have to give us any information. However, without it some features won’t work. For example, SOS alerts can’t include a location if location access is turned off. Because MemoryCare connects a person with their caregivers, it isn’t practical to use the Service anonymously or under a pseudonym, although you can use a first name or nickname in a profile.

4. How we collect it

  • From you, when you create an account, set up a profile or enter information in the app.
  • From caregivers, who may enter information about the person receiving care and about other members of the care circle.
  • From the device, through permissions you grant, such as location, motion sensors, notifications and microphone or speaker for read-aloud features.
  • From app stores, such as confirmation of a purchase or subscription (we don’t receive full card details).
  • When you contact us, for example by email.

You can change device permissions at any time in your phone’s settings.

5. How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • provide the Service, including reminders, read-aloud prompts, check-ins, brain games and the caregiver dashboard;
  • send SOS, fall, missed-dose and other alerts to the care circle;
  • show caregivers the person’s location, activity, wellbeing and trends;
  • create and manage accounts, and verify identity and authority;
  • respond to questions and support requests;
  • keep the Service safe, secure and working, including fixing bugs and preventing misuse;
  • improve the Service, using de-identified or aggregated information wherever possible; and
  • meet our legal obligations.

We will only use or disclose personal information for another purpose if you consent, if it’s a related purpose you would reasonably expect (directly related, for health information), or if the law requires or allows it, for example to lessen a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety.

Marketing: we will never use health information for marketing. We may send caregivers occasional news about MemoryCare by email. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email, in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

6. Automated features and AI

Some MemoryCare features use computer programs to make or suggest decisions automatically, using personal information:

  • Fall alerts: motion data is analysed to detect a possible fall. If there’s no response to the “Are you OK?” prompt before the countdown ends, caregivers are alerted automatically.
  • Missed-dose alerts: if a medication isn’t marked as taken within a set time, caregivers are alerted automatically.
  • AI insights: check-ins, task completion and game results are summarised by an artificial intelligence (AI) service to give caregivers a short daily summary, such as “Mood has been consistently positive this week”.

These features support caregivers. They do not make medical, financial or legal decisions, and they are not a diagnosis. AI summaries can be wrong or incomplete, and should always be checked against what you know about the person. If you have concerns about how an automated feature affected you, contact us.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we don’t share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with:

  • The care circle: caregivers linked to the person receiving care can see their tasks, medications, check-ins, alerts, location, game results and insights. Who is in the care circle is managed in the app.
  • Service providers who help us run MemoryCare, such as cloud hosting and database providers, push notification services, AI service providers, email providers and crash reporting tools. They may only use the information to provide services to us, and must protect it.
  • Apple and Google, to the extent needed to distribute the app, deliver notifications and process in-app purchases.
  • Emergency services, if you choose to call them from the app, or where we reasonably believe it’s necessary to lessen or prevent a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, courts and law enforcement, where required or authorised by law.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or restructured, provided they agree to protect the information in line with this policy.

8. Overseas storage and processing

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia. At the date of this policy, information may be stored or processed in [list countries, e.g. the United States].

Before disclosing personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to make sure the recipient handles it in line with the APPs, including through contractual protections.

9. Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, change or disclosure. These include encrypting data in transit, limiting staff access to people who need it, using secure login, and choosing service providers with strong security practices.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your login details private and let us know straight away if you think your account has been compromised.

10. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law. When an account is deleted, we delete or de-identify its personal information within a reasonable time. Copies in secure backups are removed as those backups are routinely replaced. We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

11. Accessing and correcting information

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing memorycareau@gmail.com. An authorised representative can also ask on behalf of the person receiving care.

We will respond within 30 days. We won’t charge you for making a request. If we refuse access or correction, we’ll explain why in writing (unless that would be unreasonable) and tell you how to complain. Much of the information can also be viewed and edited directly in the app.

12. Your choices

  • Permissions: turn location, motion and notification access on or off in your device settings.
  • Care circle: add or remove caregivers in the app.
  • Marketing: unsubscribe from emails at any time.
  • Delete your account: in the app, or by emailing us. Deleting the account of the person receiving care also removes the information shared with their care circle.

13. Our website

Our website doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies. It saves your “larger text” preference in your own browser so it’s remembered next time. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with Google, under Google’s privacy policy.

14. Data breaches

We comply with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to anyone, we will notify the affected people and the OAIC as the law requires, and tell them what steps to take.

15. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled personal information, please email us at memorycareau@gmail.com. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and aim to respond within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

  • Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll post the new version on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a change is significant, we’ll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
