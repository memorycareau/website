4. Limits of safety features

We work hard to make SOS, fall and missed-dose alerts reliable, but they depend on things outside our control. Alerts may be delayed or may not be sent or received if, for example:

  • the phone is switched off, out of battery, out of range or not connected to the internet;

  • a third-party service (such as a mobile network or notification service) fails.
